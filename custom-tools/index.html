Private tool

Prospect Finder

Built for Ask the Turtle. Type in a nonprofit’s name, and it reads the nonprofit’s IRS Form 990 filings, finds the right people to contact, checks each email address, and returns a spreadsheet. Every field notes its source, and anyone on a do-not-contact list stays out. It holds client data, so it has no public link; the screenshot uses placeholder records.

How a custom tool project runs

  1. Tell me what you need.The job the tool should do, who will use it, and when you need it. A short description is plenty.
  2. Agree on a price and a date.Both are fixed before I start.
  3. I research before I build.I learn how your team does the job today, and I look up anything I’m not sure of.
  4. Get the finished tool.You own the files and the source code.
  5. Keep it current.I can teach your team to update it, or keep it up to date for you.
