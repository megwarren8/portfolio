How a custom tool project runs

  1. Tell me what you need.The job the tool should do, who will use it, and when you need it. A short description is plenty.
  2. Get a fixed price and a date.Quoted by scope, before any work starts.
  3. Research comes first.I learn how your team does the job today, and I look up anything I’m not sure of.
  4. Get the finished tool.You own the files and the source code.
  5. Keep it current.I can show your team how to update it, or handle the upkeep and updates myself whenever you need them.
