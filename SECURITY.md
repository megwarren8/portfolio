# Reporting a security problem

**Email meganraewarren@gmail.com.** That is the whole process.

Most of the people reading this are my own students. If you found something,
I would much rather hear it than not. You are not in trouble, and reading the
source of a page is not an attack. This file exists because a student told me
my code had no license, and he was right, so it seemed fair to also tell people
where to send the next thing they find.

## What is worth reporting

- Anything that exposes a solutions key, a teacher page, or a Lesson Studio
  without the password.
- Anything that lets one person see another person's work, or act as them.
- A way past a password gate, or a way to make a gate let you in.
- Anything that would let someone change what other readers see.

## What is already known, and is not a finding

- **The books are readable, downloadable and free.** All of the text, figures
  and exercises are meant to be in your browser. That is the design, not a leak.
- **The page source is meant to be read.** Everything a book needs is embedded
  in one file on purpose, so it works with no internet. Please do read it.
- **Answers to exercises are in the student book on purpose.** Hint ladders and
  worked solutions are part of how the book teaches. The teacher-only material
  is the separate, password-gated Solutions Key.

## What not to do

Please do not run automated scanners or load tests, and please do not try
anything during school hours. A class may be using the site. You do not need
to prove an issue at scale for me to take it seriously; a clear description of
what you did and what you saw is plenty.

## What happens next

I will reply. If you are one of my students and you would like credit for the
find, say so and I will name you, with your permission, in the fix's commit.
