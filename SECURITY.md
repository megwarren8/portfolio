# Reporting a security problem

Email **contact@megan-warren.com**.

Please include what you did and what you saw. A description is enough; there is
no need to demonstrate an issue at scale.

## In scope

- Access to a solutions key, a teacher page or a Lesson Studio without the password.
- Access to another person's submitted work, or the ability to act as them.
- Any bypass of a password gate.
- Any way to alter what other readers are served.

## Not in scope

- The books are published free to read and to download. Their content being
  available is intended, not a defect.
- Hint ladders and worked solutions inside a student book are intended. The
  teacher material is the separate, password-gated Solutions Key.

## Please do not

Run automated scanners or load tests against these sites, or test during school
hours, when a class may be using them.
