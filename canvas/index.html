Unit 2 hub: The Unit Circle

By the end of Unit 2 you will be able to:

  • Before solving a two-sides-and-an-angle (SSA) setup, count its triangles by comparing side aaa with the height h=bsin⁡Ah = b\sin Ah=bsinA. Then solve each possible triangle, catching the second triangle's angle as the supplement B′=180∘−BB' = 180^\circ - BB′=180∘−B.
  • Measure rotation the circle's own way. Set an angle in standard position, name its coterminal angles, and define the radian. Convert between degrees and radians, and find the arc length and sector area a central angle cuts off.
  • Read the sine and cosine of any angle, not merely an acute one, as the coordinates where its terminal side meets the unit circle. Take tangent as that side's slope. Off the axes, take every sign from the quadrant the angle lies in. Mnemonic: ASTC, All Star Trig Class. Take every absolute value from a reference angle θ′\theta'θ′.

  • Define cosecant, secant, and cotangent as the reciprocals of sine, cosine, and tangent. Evaluate them at the 16 unit-circle angles, and locate where each one is undefined. Keep the reciprocal 1sin⁡θ\frac{1}{\sin\theta}sinθ1​ apart from the inverse sine sin⁡−1\sin^{-1}sin−1.
  • Read the quotient identities tan⁡θ=sin⁡θcos⁡θ\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}tanθ=cosθsinθ​ and cot⁡θ=cos⁡θsin⁡θ\cot\theta = \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}cotθ=sinθcosθ​ straight off the coordinates. Check your answer

b≈15.027b \approx 15.027b≈15.027 cm.

Check your answer

Opposite side =8sin⁡35∘≈4.589= 8\sin 35^\circ \approx 4.589=8sin35∘≈4.589.
