1:1 AI Coaching

Prefer to learn to do it yourself?

Megan Warren is a teacher of twelve years who coaches people 1:1 on using AI tools themselves, in person in Boston, Massachusetts, or remotely by video call anywhere else. Watching someone else use a tool does not teach most people something they can repeat tomorrow. I start by learning how you already work and build a plan around it. Then I sit next to you while you drive. You leave able to do it again without me.

How it works

1. First conversation
A real conversation, fifteen to twenty minutes, free. Where you are now, what you've already tried, what stuck, what didn't, and what you want to be able to do on your own.
2. Your game plan
A short, written plan built around that conversation, a lesson plan, not a template. You see exactly what we're covering and why before you spend a dollar.
3. Guided practice
Forty-five to sixty minutes, hands-on. You drive, I scaffold: enough support that you never get stuck, never so much that I do it for you.

Questions people actually ask

What does it cost?
The first conversation is free. After that, $300 flat covers your written game plan and one guided session of 45 to 60 minutes. Extra sessions are $150 each. Nothing to buy up front, no subscription.
In person or remote?
Both. In person if you're within about five miles of downtown Boston, Massachusetts, and over Zoom everywhere else. The format does not change the price.
Do I need a technical background?
No. The plan is built around how you already work, not around a fixed curriculum. Most people arrive having tried a chatbot a few times and wanting to know what they're missing.
Which AI tools do you teach?
Whichever ones fit the work you actually do. The tool choice follows from your first conversation instead of being decided before I've met you.
How is this different from a course?
You drive. A video shows you someone else being fluent, which is why most people can't repeat it the next day. Here you do the work, with enough scaffolding that you never get stuck.
Who's the coach?
Megan Warren, a math teacher and tutor of twelve years who also designs and builds software on commission. The teaching is the qualification.
