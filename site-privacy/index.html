Privacy

This site keeps nothing about you.

megan-warren.com is a portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no ads. This page says what little happens when you visit, and where your choices live.

Last updated October 31, 2026.

What loads when you open a page

Every page, font, picture, and script comes from megan-warren.com itself. Nothing loads from an ad network, an analytics service, or a social media site. Cloudflare Pages hosts the site and answers each request the way any web host does. I keep no visitor logs and no record of who has been here.

Your light or dark choice

The day and night switch in the header saves your choice in your own browser's storage, so the site opens the same way next time. That setting never leaves your device, and I cannot see it. To clear it, delete this site's data in your browser's settings.

If you email or message me

Email to contact@megan-warren.com lands in my inbox. I use what you send only to reply and to do the work you ask about, and I never add you to a mailing list. Messages sent through LinkedIn travel through LinkedIn under its own privacy policy.

The free books

The seven free math books at books.megan-warren.com remember a few reading settings on your device. They have their own privacy page that covers the books, their slides, and their games.

Questions

Email contact@megan-warren.com. I reply within two business days.
