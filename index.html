Questions clients ask

Does it only work for math?
No. Math is one of the hardest subjects to typeset, so it makes a good test. Onboarding guides, study guides, and training manuals are built the same way.
Is this a course platform?
No. Each guide is a file you own outright, with no subscription, no login, and nothing to renew.
Couldn’t I just build this with AI?
AI can get you a draft, and I use it in my own work. What I add is knowing what to verify. My Trigonometry book passes 85 checks before every update goes live, including a computer re-solve of 906 answers.
Who writes it?
Either of us. Bring finished content and I’ll build it, or bring an outline and I’ll write it too.

Work with me

I taught teenagers for more than twelve years. That meant daily troubleshooting with learners of every kind: finding where each one got stuck and working through the frustration until the math made sense. Now I do instructional design and technical writing from Boston, taking paid remote work for companies, nonprofits, small businesses, publishers, and schools. You work with me directly, from the first message to the finished files.

  1. Tell me what you need.The subject, who will read it, and roughly how long. An outline is plenty.
  2. Get a fixed price and a date.Quoted by scope, before any work starts.
  3. Research comes first.I study your readers and your field, and I look up anything I’m not sure of.
  4. Get the finished work.You own the files and the source code.
  5. Keep it current.I can show your team how to update it, or handle the upkeep and updates myself whenever you need them.
