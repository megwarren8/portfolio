Questions clients ask

Does it only work for math?
No. Math is one of the hardest subjects to typeset, so it makes a good test. Onboarding guides, study guides, and training manuals are built the same way.
Is this a course platform?
No. Each guide is a file you own outright, with no subscription, no login, and nothing to renew. Canvas pages live in your Canvas; everything else is a file you own.
Couldn’t I just build this with AI?
The work is knowing what to check. Every update to my Trigonometry book passes 85 automated checks before it goes live, including a computer re-solve of 906 answers. AI can get you a draft, and I use it in my own work. What I add is the checking.
Who writes it?
Either of us. Bring finished content and I’ll build it, or bring an outline and I’ll write it too.
