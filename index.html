Questions clients ask

Couldn’t I just build this with AI?
AI can write a first draft, and I use it in my own work. What it can’t do is tell you when the draft is wrong. Every update to my Trigonometry book has to pass 86 automated checks before it goes live, and one of them has a computer re-derive the answer to all 906 exercises that have one. That checking is the part you hire me for.
Does it only work for math?
No. Math is one of the hardest subjects to lay out on a page. That makes it a good test. Onboarding guides, study guides, and training manuals are built the same way.
Is this a course platform?
No. You get files you own outright: no subscription, no login, nothing to renew. Canvas pages go into your own Canvas course.
Who writes it?
Either of us. Bring finished content and I’ll build it, or bring an outline and I’ll write it too.
