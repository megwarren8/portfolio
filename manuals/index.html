How I build each training manual

The same way I built my seven free books.

  • Steps in orderOne action per step, in the order the reader does them, so nobody has to jump ahead to find the part they need.
  • Worked examplesA finished example beside each new idea, so readers see what “done” looks like before they try it.
  • Hints where readers get stuckHints that build one step at a time, placed at the spots where a new reader stalls.
  • Print it your wayA print menu with a choice for every reader, from the full section to the formula sheet alone, with or without an answer key.
  • Read aloudA read-aloud button that highlights each passage as it’s read and speaks every math expression.
  • Checked before it shipsAutomated checks run before each update to my books goes live. The questions below have the numbers.

Questions clients ask about manuals

Does it only work for math?
No. Math is one of the hardest subjects to typeset, so it makes a good test. Onboarding guides, study guides, and training manuals are built the same way.
Is this a course platform?
No. Each guide is a file you own outright, with no subscription, no login, and nothing to renew. Canvas pages live in your Canvas; everything else is a file you own.
Couldn’t I just build this with AI?
The work is knowing what to check. Every update to my Trigonometry book passes 85 automated checks before it goes live, including a computer re-solve of 906 answers. AI can get you a draft, and I use it in my own work. What I add is the checking.
Who writes it?
Either of us. Bring finished content and I’ll build it, or bring an outline and I’ll write it too.

How a project runs

  1. Tell me what you need.The subject, who will read it, and roughly how long. An outline is plenty.
  2. Get a fixed price and a date.Quoted by scope, before any work starts.
  3. Research comes first.I study your readers and your field, and I look up anything I’m not sure of.
  4. Get the finished work.You own the files and the source code.
  5. Keep it current.I can show your team how to update it, or handle the upkeep and updates myself whenever you need them.
