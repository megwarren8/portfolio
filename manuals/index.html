How I build a manual

The same way I built my seven free books.

  • Steps in orderOne action per step, in the sequence the reader follows. Nobody hunts for the next part.
  • Worked examplesA finished example beside each new idea, so that readers see what “done” looks like before they try it.
  • Hints where readers stallEach hint gives one more step, not the answer, and sits where new readers tend to stop.
  • Print it your wayA print menu with a choice for every reader, from the full section to the formula sheet alone, with or without an answer key.
  • Read aloudA read-aloud button that highlights each passage as it reads, math included.
  • Checked before it shipsEvery answer, spelling, and page layout is checked before an update goes live.

Questions clients ask about manuals

Couldn’t I just build this with AI?
AI can write a page. It can’t tell which step will lose your new hire, and that is where a guide works or fails. I spent thirteen years finding the exact spot where people get stuck. I write for that spot: what comes first, which example to show, what to leave out. Before I write, I learn how your team does the job, so the guide fits your work, not a generic version of it. I use AI for drafts. Knowing what your readers need is what you hire me for.
Do you only do math work?
No. My samples are math books because math is what I taught. The method carries over to any subject or process: one action per step, a worked example for each new idea, hints where readers stall, and checks before anything ships.
Is this a course platform?
No. There is no subscription, no login, and nothing to renew. Guides and tools are files you own outright, and Canvas pages go into your own Canvas.
Who writes it?
Either of us. Send finished content, and I’ll build it. Send an outline, and I’ll write it too. Either way, you get one fixed price, quoted from the scope before any work starts.

How a project runs

  1. Tell me what you need.The subject, who will read it, and roughly how long. An outline is plenty.
  2. Agree on a price and a date.Both are fixed before I start.
  3. I research before I write.I learn who your readers are and what they already know, and I look up anything I’m not sure of.
  4. Get the finished work.You own all the files.
  5. Keep it current.I can teach your team to update it, or keep it up to date for you.
